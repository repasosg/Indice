📖 Teoría del Past Simple ¡Empieza aquí!
¿Qué es el Past Simple? Lo usamos para hablar de acciones que ya terminaron en el pasado.
Palabras clave: yesterday, last week, last year, ago, in 2020...

✅ Frases Afirmativas

Subject + verb (past) + ...

Verbos regulares → añade -ed: walk → walked, play → played

Verbos irregulares → forma especial: go → went, eat → ate

🔵 I played football yesterday.

🔵 She went to the park last Sunday.

❌ Frases Negativas

Subject + didn't + verb (base form) + ...

🔴 I didn't play football yesterday.

🔴 She didn't go to the park.

❓ Preguntas

Did + subject + verb (base form) + ...?

❓ Did you play football? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
