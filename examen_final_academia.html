🕐 Telling the Time & Prepositions of Time
In, At, On — and how to tell the time!
⏰ Telling the Time:
1:00 It's one o'clock   1:15 It's quarter past one
1:30 It's half past one   1:45 It's quarter to two
2:10 It's ten past two   2:50 It's ten to three
📅 Prepositions of Time:
IN months, seasons, years → in January, in summer, in 2024
AT times, special times → at 3 o'clock, at night, at the weekend
ON days, dates → on Monday, on 5th June, on Christmas Day

🏠 There is & There are
Affirmative, negative and questions!
📌 Rules:
THERE IS singular → There is a cat on the sofa.
THERE ARE plural → There are two books on the table.
THERE ISN'T negative singular → There isn't a TV in the kitchen.
THERE AREN'T negative plural → There aren't any chairs here.

📍 Prepositions of Place
Where is everything?
📌 Prepositions of Place:
IN inside → The cat is in the box.
ON on top → The book is on the table.
UNDER below → The dog is under the chair.
NEXT TO beside → The lamp is next to the sofa.
BEHIND at the back → The tree is behind the house.
IN FRONT OF at the front → The car is in front of the house.
BETWEEN in the middle of two things → The shop is between the school and the park.

📝 Present Simple
Habits, routines, and facts!
📌 Present Simple Rules:
I / You / We / They → base verb → I play, They eat
He / She / It → verb + S → He plays, She eats
Special: watch→watches | go→goes | have→has | study→studies
NEGATIVE: don't / doesn't + verb → I don't like. She doesn't go.
QUESTION: Do / Does + subject + verb? → Do you play? Does she go?

💪 Can & Can't
Ability — what can you do?
📌 CAN / CAN'T Rules:
CAN ability → I can swim. She can speak French.
CAN'T / CANNOT no ability → He can't fly. They can't drive.
⚡ Important: CAN never changes! → She can (NOT she cans!)
QUESTION: Can + subject + verb? → Can you swim? Can she dance?
Short answers: Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
AffirmativeNegativeQuestion
I can swimI can't swimCan I swim?
She can singShe can't singCan she sing?
They can runThey can't runCan they run?

🎨 Adjectives & Nouns
Describing things!
📌 Adjective + Noun Order in English:
In English, adjectives come BEFORE the noun!
✅ a big house   ❌ a house big
✅ a red car   ❌ a car red
Adjectives describe: size (big, small), colour (red, blue), age (old, new), shape (round, flat), opinion (nice, horrible)

🏃 Present Continuous
What is happening RIGHT NOW?
📌 Present Continuous = BE + VERB+ING:
I am playing → I'm playing
He / She / It is reading → He's reading
We / You / They are running → They're running
NEGATIVE: I'm not, He isn't, They aren't + verb+ing
QUESTION: Am I? Is he? Are they? + verb+ing?
Spelling: run→running | swim→swimming | write→writing (drop the e)
SubjectAffirmativeNegativeQuestion
II'm eatingI'm not eatingAm I eating?
He/She/ItShe's dancingShe isn't dancingIs she dancing?
We/You/TheyThey're runningThey aren't runningAre they running?
